# Development Roadmap

[Back to Purgatory Post](README.md)

The milestones below describe the work, not a release calendar. **Completed** means the initial milestone has a working implementation; its balance and presentation can still change.

| Stage | Status | Milestone |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Concept & Prototype | Completed | Turn the premise of an afterlife post office into a playable desk puzzle. |
| 2. Core Gameplay | Completed | Play through a working week using timed actions, incoming files, a deck of Tools and an end-of-run outcome. |
| 3. Strategic Systems | Baseline implemented | Connect deck building, the shop, pay, scoring and persistent Dispositions. The expanded catalog is implemented in the experimental build and remains in playtest. |
| 4. Gameplay Expansion | Current phase | Replay full weeks with different builds; check payouts, targeting and the transitions between deliveries, shifts and days. |
| 5. Product Refinement | Planned, partially underway | Refine card text, information hierarchy and the office's visual language. Further content and visual work remain ahead. |
| 6. Beta Testing | Planned | Test whether new players can learn the game without guidance and complete runs; collect reproducible feedback. |
| 7. Public Release | Planned | Prepare the public build and player-facing materials after reviewing beta findings. |

## What comes before beta?

A week needs to be repeatable without a blocking error. A new player should be able to understand a loss, read a reward before choosing it and tell what the next action will cost. Those are more useful readiness signals than the size of the card catalog.
